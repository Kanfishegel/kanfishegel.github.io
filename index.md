# About Me

My name is **Yanfei Zhu**.

I recently received my M.A. in Mathematics from the University of Wisconsin–Madison in May 2026. I will be applying for 2027 Fall PhD programs in Mathematics. I am interested in using algebraic and topological methods to study geometric problems, especially those involving manifolds. My current research focuses on equivariant homotopy theory and homological stability. I am also interested in optimization on manifolds.

## Preprints

<ul>
<li>
 <strong><a href="https://kanfishegel.github.io/file/C_2.pdf" target="_blank">Two Lifted Actions on Oriented Configuration Spaces: Stabilization
and Bredon Homology</a></strong>
 <details>
 <summary>Abstract</summary>
We study the two lifts of a reflection to oriented configuration spaces of Euclidean space, with fixed subspace of dimension at least two. We describe their fixed-point spaces and construct commuting stabilization maps u and v that add a fixed point and a free orbit, respectively: u preserves each lift, while v exchanges them. Combining both lifts and all configuration sizes, we prove that Bredon homology with constant integral coefficients is finitely generated in every degree over ℤ[u, v], and over ℤ[u, v<sup>2</sup>] for each lift separately. We also compute the degree-zero module explicitly, exhibiting 2-torsion on which u has nonzero kernel for arbitrarily large configuration sizes; for unordered configurations, the corresponding map is injective in degree zero.
 </details>
 </li>


<li>
 <strong><a href="https://kanfishegel.github.io/file/C_q.pdf" target="_blank">The RO(C<sub>q</sub>)-Graded Cohomology of the Complete Flag Variety of ℂ[C<sub>q</sub>]</a></strong>
 <details>
 <summary>Abstract</summary>
Revisiting our earlier computation for C<sub>3</sub>, we found that the method extends to cyclic groups of any odd prime order. For C<sub>q</sub> acting on the complete flag variety of its complex regular representation, we compute the genuine RO(C<sub>q</sub>)-graded cohomology ring with constant F<sub>q</sub> coefficients, giving a presentation over the full coefficient ring, a free Schubert basis, and a Monk formula. We determine the matrix of restriction to the fixed flags, identifying its nonzero square-zero kernel and the higher Euler-class divisibility conditions describing its image. We also compute the quotient of the solutions of root-curve congruences by this image. Finally, we describe the cohomology Mackey functor and the mod-q cohomology ring of the orbit space, whose positive-degree products vanish.
 </details>
 </li>

</ul>
